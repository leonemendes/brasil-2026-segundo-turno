Brazil presidential runoffs 2018 · 2022 · 2026 · TSE boletins de urna

Is there a pattern in the order of the count?

Every urna result has a time stamp for when the TSE received it. We replay the three runoffs urna by urna, in the real order and in random orders, and compare them.

The claim

In a video, Prof. Marcelo Trindade says he took 472 urnas from the TSE site and plotted the running vote share. He says parts of the curve rise in a straight line and then drop again and again, and that this cannot happen when the order of arrival is random. He says an algorithm caused the drops and that Flávio Bolsonaro would have about 65%. Report of the video.

The numbers

Right-wing candidate share of valid votes (Bolsonaro 2018 and 2022, Flávio Bolsonaro 2026) after a given percentage of urnas, in the real receive order.

Method and sources

  • Data: TSE open data, "Boletim de urna" files for each runoff (2026, 2022, 2018). Section totals match the official national results exactly in all three years.
  • Order: the column DT_BU_RECEBIDO (time the TSE received the urna result) in 2022 and 2026. The 2018 file has no such column.
  • Same-order replay: sections with the same state, municipality, zone and section number in all three years ( of the 2026 sections).
  • Random band: 200 random orders of all sections, 2.5th to 97.5th percentile. Random samples use a fixed seed, so a rerun gives the same lines.
  • Share = right-wing candidate votes ÷ (both candidates' votes). Blank and null votes are left out, as in the official result.
  • We did not watch the video itself. The claim above comes from the linked report. On 25 October the TSE integrity test compared paper ballots with urna records and found no difference.
  • Code: scripts/count-order.ts in the brasil-2026-segundo-turno repository.
