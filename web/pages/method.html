How this was built

  • Round 1, 2018 and 2022: the TSE "boletim de urna" open-data files. One row per section and candidate, with the urna's open and close times. The totals match the official results exactly.
  • 2026 runoff: the TSE results service. National and state totals match the official result (Lula 64,256,232, Flávio 59,826,041). Municipal files are fetched slowly to respect the TSE's rate limit, so the map fills in over the day.
  • Close time: urnas record local time. Each municipality's official close is its usual opening hour plus 9 hours (8:00 to 17:00). In 2022 and 2026 that is 17:00 Brasília time everywhere; in 2018 it was 17:00 local time.
  • "Open 49+ minutes": the urna closed at 17:49 Brasília or later. 17:49 is when the TSE told sections still open about the extension.
  • Queue or court order: the urna log has a time for every vote, but not the candidate. Votes that continue after 17:00 with less than 5 minutes between them are the 17:00 queue. Votes after a pause of 5 minutes or more are new arrivals that only the extension allowed.
  • Abroad: the TSE gives each overseas city a code and a Portuguese name only. The city positions on the world map were added by hand and are approximate. All vote numbers are the TSE's.
  • Map shapes: IBGE municipal boundaries (tbrugz/geodata-br), simplified; world countries from Natural Earth (world-atlas). A few very new municipalities have no shape.
  • Votes after 17:00: counted from the log of every urna that closed at 17:45 Brasília or later, downloaded one by one from the TSE results service. The list of those urnas comes from the independent analysis apolinario/urnas-tardias; every count on this page is ours, made from the TSE logs, and matches that analysis except for a few votes logged at exactly 17:00:00. Urnas that closed before 17:45 were not re-downloaded; their late votes are the normal end-of-day queue.
  • Count patterns: the runoff boletim de urna files for 2018, 2022 and 2026. In 2022 and 2026 the column DT_BU_RECEBIDO gives the time the TSE received each urna; the 2018 file has no such column. The 2026-order replay uses the sections with the same state, municipality, zone and section number in all three years. Random orders use a fixed seed, so a rebuild gives the same lines. Built by scripts/analysis/count-order.ts.
  • Forensic report check: scripts/analysis/report-check.ts reads the 2022 and 2026 runoff boletim de urna files and recomputes the report's tables: the split by the close time the urna records (local time), the sections closed after 19:00, the voters released without biometrics (QT_ELEI_BIOM_SEM_HABILITACAO in 2026, QT_ELEITORES_BIOMETRIA_NH in 2022) and the near-unanimous sections. It runs the same checks on 2022, which had no extension.
  • What is still pending: the TSE's bulk boletim de urna file for the runoff, with every urna's close time. When it is out, the runoff line in the close-time chart appears and the list of late urnas can be checked against it.
